How do I read chess moves like "Qxf7#"?

Each piece has a letter: K king, Q queen, R rook, B bishop, N knight (pawns have no letter). Then comes the square it moves to, like f7. An x means a capture, + means check, and # means checkmate. So "Qxf7#" means "queen captures on f7, checkmate!" Learn more.
